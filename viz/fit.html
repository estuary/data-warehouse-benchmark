Which warehouses served a workload like yours?

There is no best data warehouse; the right one depends on your workload and your requirements. Three questions decide which of the results below apply to you, and the settings after them refine the answer:

  1. Do your queries join tables or scan one wide table? Reports built by a business intelligence tool over a sales or finance model are join queries. Dashboards that count events, page views or telemetry from one wide table are scan queries.
  2. How many people will have a query running at the same moment? This is typically the subset of your user base that queries at one time, during peak hours.
  3. What hourly spend would you approve? The budget field below compares the engines at the spend you set.

Size the warehouse against the volume of data people query. That is often a reporting layer of a few hundred gigabytes or less, much smaller than everything the company stores. Every result below is one unit of compute at one size, measured with automatic scaling switched off. Treat the answer as a shortlist to test on your own workload.
